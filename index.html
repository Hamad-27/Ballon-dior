THE HISTORY

The story of football's most prestigious individual award

1956 — The Beginning

France Football introduced the Ballon d'Or in 1956. English footballer Stanley Matthews became its first winner.

European Era

Initially, the award was limited to European players playing for European clubs.

1995 — A New Era

Players of any nationality playing for European clubs became eligible.

2007 — Worldwide

The award opened to players from around the world, regardless of the league in which they played.

2018 — Women's Award

The Women's Ballon d'Or was introduced, recognising outstanding achievements in women's football.

2020 — No Award

The Ballon d'Or was cancelled in 2020 because of the extraordinary circumstances of the pandemic.
